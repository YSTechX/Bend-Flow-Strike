# Bend Flow Strike — Choose your element. Win the duel.

**A best‑of‑5 elemental duel: choose Air, Water, Earth, or Fire and challenge your opponent.**

### Rules
- **Elements:** Air → Water → Earth → Fire
- **Win conditions:**
  - **Air beats Earth** — wind erodes and destabilizes ground.
  - **Water beats Fire** — water douses and extinguishes fire.
  - **Earth beats Water** — ground blocks and absorbs water.
  - **Fire beats Air** — flames consume and scatter wind.
- **Format:** Best‑of‑5; first to **3** wins the duel.
- **Ties:** If both choose the same element, the round is a tie and neither competitor gains a win.
